<p >
I work as an AI Researcher in the Computer Science division at Honda R&D Japan. Prior to which I finished my masters in Electrical Engineering from IIT Bombay with a specialisation in Control and Computing. During my time at IIT Bombay, I worked as a Project Research Assistant under <a href="https://www.ee.iitb.ac.in/~sc/" target="_blank">Prof. Subhasis Chaudhuri</a> in <a href="http://www.ee.iitb.ac.in/~viplab/" target="_blank">Vision and Image Processing Lab</a>. I have also worked very closely with <a href="https://biplab-banerjee.github.io/" target="_blank">Prof. Biplab Banerjee</a> of Centre of Studies in Resources Engineering, IIT Bombay.  <br>
My research interests include artificial intelligence and computer vision.
