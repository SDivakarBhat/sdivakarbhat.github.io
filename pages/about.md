I am an AI researcher at Honda R&amp;D Japan and a PhD student at The University of Tokyo. My work sits at the intersection of reliable computer vision, adaptive learning, and real-world autonomy. I am interested in building vision systems that remain robust under domain shift, class imbalance, continual updates, and deployment constraints.

Before joining Honda R&amp;D Japan, I completed my master&rsquo;s in Electrical Engineering with a specialization in Control and Computing at IIT Bombay, where I worked with [Prof. Subhasis Chaudhuri](https://www.ee.iitb.ac.in/~sc/) at the Vision and Image Processing Lab and collaborated closely with [Prof. Biplab Banerjee](https://biplab-banerjee.github.io/). My earlier work focused on continual learning, incremental learning, remote sensing, and few-shot co-segmentation.

I am currently based in Tokyo. Outside research, I enjoy fitness, running, cycling, trekking, reading, sketching, writing, and exploring questions around dharma, discipline, and purposeful living.
