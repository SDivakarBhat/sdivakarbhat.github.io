# The Maiden Metric Century Ride - My first 100km cycling experience.
_“The best rides are the ones where you bite off much more than you can chew, and live through it - Doug Bradbury"_

The quote beautifully summarises my complete experience of cycling from Kochi to Marari Beach and back, for my first metric century ride. It all started off in early August, from one of those typical covid era lethargic days. I was wasting my time scrolling through random reels, youtube shorts and satisfying my curiosity asking Google about extremely weird and random questions I had on a wide array of topics from trivial day to day life to mind boggling conspiracy theories. (We will talk about it in maybe some other post.) In the process I found myself reading some [old news article](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/Mangalore/eight-riders-clinch-the-coveted-super-randonneur-title-after-completing-a-600-km-ride-in-40-hours/article33697002.ece) about a bunch of cycling enthusiast completing around 600kms of ride here in Kerala. The sheer amount of kilometers they logged in ignited my curiosity about pursuing long cycling tours and added a word to which I was pretty unfamilar till then to my vocabulary, ['Randonneuring'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randonneuring). Little did I know that this random news snippet was going to change my life forever, opening up a new chapter in my life.
