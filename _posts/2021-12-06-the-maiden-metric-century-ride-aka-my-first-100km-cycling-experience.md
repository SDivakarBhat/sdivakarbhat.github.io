# The Maiden Metric Century Ride - My first 100km cycling experience.
_“The best rides are the ones where you bite off much more than you can chew, and live through it - Doug Bradbury"_

The quote beautifully summarises my complete experience of cycling from my hometown, [Kochi](https://www.google.com/search?q=Kochi&oq=Kochi&aqs=chrome..69i57j69i61j69i60l2.4405j0j1&sourceid=chrome&ie=UTF-8) a bustling city in Kerala to the quite and beautiful [Marari Beach](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Marari+beach/@9.60075,76.2807489,14z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x3b0887be9871a19b:0x7f54df9416daa008!8m2!3d9.6007506!4d76.2982585) and back, for my first metric century ride. It all started off in early August, from one of those typical covid era lethargic days. I was wasting my time scrolling through random reels, youtube shorts and satisfying my curiosity asking Google about extremely weird and random questions I had on a wide array of topics from trivial day to day life to mind boggling conspiracy theories. (We will talk about it in maybe some other post.) In the process I found myself reading some [old news article](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/Mangalore/eight-riders-clinch-the-coveted-super-randonneur-title-after-completing-a-600-km-ride-in-40-hours/article33697002.ece) about a bunch of cycling enthusiast completing around 600kms of ride here in Kerala. The sheer amount of kilometers they logged in ignited my curiosity about pursuing long cycling tours and added a word to which I was pretty unfamilar till then to my vocabulary, ['Randonneuring'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randonneuring). Little did I know that this random news snippet was going to change my life forever, opening up a new chapter in my life. 

What followed was a series of obsessive reading on randonneuring, AUDAX and long distance biking, nourishing my burning desire to attempt long distance cycling myself and gradually become a randonneur. For a person who found it mentally tiring to cycle even a distance of around 20kms despite having a decent physique, it was audacious and I sounded like a blind fool for aspiring to be a randonneur out of the blue. But as they say, and time and again as I have experienced, its foolish confidence along with the naiveness with which you go off to work your ass off persistently that has always helped me to make progress. Thus I decided to approach this gradually riding on my old fixed gear Hercules Apache cycle, so as to test out my ability to endure exhaustion and keep going and started off my journey with a rather small 20+ kms ,followed by a 60+ kms, and finally making it to the maiden metric century ride.

As typical it is for a [Kochiite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_Kochi), I chose to ride to Fort Kochi beach from my home making the to and fro ride more than 20 kms. Eventhough it has been quite a long break since I cycled for more than 20km, but despite that I was sure it was not going to be a challenging task and hence was looking forward to enjoy the ride. It was a picturesque route and offered a very refreshing riding experience topped off with slight drizzle. I spend some time at the Fort Kochi beach enjoying that strange sense of calmness settling onto you, while watching the chaotic and loud waves crashing on the rocks. The roundtrip as recorded by strava was around 24.71 km in 1h 25m time, and as expected it was more of a refreshing ride than a daunting one serving as the perfect confidence booster.

After my previous ride to Fort Kochi, I wanted to level up a bit. Cconsidering the fact that I was not in a bad shape and was able to log around 5km run every other day I decided to cycle to [Munambam beach](https://www.webindia123.com/city/kerala/kochi-ernakulam/destinations/dams-waterfalls/munambambeach.htm). One of the perks of living in a coastal city in Kerala like Kochi is that you have access to a lot of beautiful beaches with even more charming routes to reach there.
I was aware that this would be a demanding ride as it was my longest ride till now and also as the route was full of small and large bridges over numerous waterways connecting the islands. Therefore I planned to start early and also ensured that I am full prepared with enough water and snacks. The cycle also started getting gradually fitted with accessories like bottel holder, better seat cover, tail light, etc, based on the experience I gained from each ride.
